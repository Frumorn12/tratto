Google Docs, live: a linked note is the Google Doc. Its text shows under your ink and can be edited from the tablet; handwriting and drawings sit in front of the text and follow it. Strokes reach the document every couple of seconds even if you never stop, and the note's name becomes the document's name. Tilted pen: strokes start and end even with your palm on the screen, and light or tilted strokes no longer vanish.
